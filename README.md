# Welcome to the IGEL 2024 visualization

This interactive globe shows launch emissions and launch vehicle trajectories for the year 2024. All data is part of the Inventory of Global Emissions by Launchers (IGEL) 2024 and includes 95% of all propellant burned in oribtal launches in 2024

The colored tiles on the surface represent the total emission mass in the selected atmospheric layer and chemical species. The color represents the emission mass.

The colored trajectory lines show the ascent path of each launch vehicle. Dashed lines represent booster descent trajectories. The color represents the launch vehicle.

Click emission cells or trajectories for additional information. Use the legend in the bottom left corner to interpret vehicle colors and the emission mass color scale.

The plot is interactive and the following panels are available:

    Options: In the top right corner, you can expand the options menu. Here you can adjust the shown data. You can select the shown atmospheric Layer and emissions Species. You can filter by different Time windows, Launch Vehicles, trajectory Segments, or individual Launches. You can also hide the emissions or trajectories completely.
    Legend: In the bottom left corner, you can hide or expand the legend.
    Plots: On the right side in the middle, you can expand or hide two plots. These show the total emission mass and the altitude distribution of the currently selected launches, species, and layers.
    Toolbar: In the top right corner, you can find the Help button to open this window, the Home button to reset the perspective, and the Globe button to change the map perspective.
    Rotation: In the bottom right corner, you can find a button to toggle the rotation of the globe.
